What it is actually like to play here

Parking in Tampa

The City of Tampa publishes nothing about parking at any of the sixteen venues on this page. The word does not appear on the pickleball page, whose cards carry a label, a name, an address and a count and very little else, and the recreation-centres page gives each centre a phone number and a "Features:" line without mentioning a lot. This site does not fill that gap from a map. What the City does publish shapes the trip in other ways. Twelve of the sixteen venues are parks or a pavilion listed under "Outdoor Pickleball", and four are community centres whose courts are inside a building and open only at the session times the City prints - so at Barksdale, Cordelia B Hunt, Loretta Ingraham and Port Tampa the question is not where to leave the car but whether the gym is open at all. The recreation-centres page gives each of those centres a phone number, and for a court the City itself says was "closed for repairs through June 1", the number is worth more than a parking note. For the outdoor parks the addresses are the useful thing, and they are worth reading carefully: the Census geocoder answered four of them by changing or dropping a compass direction, and this site checked each against OpenStreetMap before publishing the address the City prints. Restrooms, like parking, are not mentioned anywhere by the City for these venues.

When the courts are busy

Tampa states hours for two of its twelve outdoor venues and for the pavilion, and for none of the other nine. Madison Street Park and Vila Brothers Park are "Open daily from sunrise to sunset" on their cards. The New Tampa Sports Pavilion prints facility hours - "Monday-Friday, 4:30-9 pm / Saturday-Sunday, 8 am-9 pm" - the only outdoor hours in the city that run to a clock time rather than to sunset. Cuscaden Park carries the one scheduled outdoor session the City publishes, "Senior open play Wed 9-11 am", a fact about who is on its six courts on a Wednesday morning rather than an hours line. Al Barnes, Copeland, Davis Islands, Forest Hills, Highland Pines, Julian B Lane, Macfarlane and Skyview have no stated hours, and because the City states no lighting for any venue in Tampa, this site cannot say whether evening play is possible at any of them. Indoors the picture reverses: all five indoor venues come with a session schedule, and the sessions are narrow. Barksdale runs weekday afternoons: Monday 1-3 pm with free beginner lessons, then 3-5:30 pm, and Tuesday to Friday 2-5:30 pm. Forest Hills runs Monday 10 am-12:30 pm, Wednesday 10 am-1 pm and Friday 6-7:30 pm. Loretta Ingraham runs Monday and Friday 6:30-8:30 pm until summer camp begins, then Mondays only from June 2. Cordelia B Hunt runs Tuesday and Thursday evenings in two versions split at June 1. Port Tampa runs Tuesday and Thursday 6-9 pm, with a repair closure on the same card. Two cards say "Play resumes after Summer Camp ends" and none of the dates carries a year, on a page the City dates 28 October 2025. Read the schedules as the City's last statement rather than this week's promise, and ring the centre first.

Court conditions

The City names no surface for any pickleball court in Tampa. No card says asphalt, concrete, acrylic or cushioned, and the recreation-centres page describes the buildings - gymnasium, fitness room, computer lab, pool - without describing floors. What the City does distinguish, on every card, is indoor from outdoor. Twelve venues are labelled "Outdoor Pickleball" and the community-centre courts sit under a heading that reads "Indoor Pickleball"; the label and the heading are the City's own words, so every Tampa venue carries an indoor-or-outdoor answer. Beyond that, two outdoor cards say something about what is underfoot. Davis Islands has "2 multi-purpose courts", the City's own word rather than pickleball courts, which means a court that may be lined or set up for something else when you arrive. Julian B Lane Riverfront Park has "2 permanent courts" and "4 portable net courts", so two of its six have nets that are always there and four are laid out for nets that are not. Every other outdoor card just says courts, and this site does not read dedicated into that. Indoors, three of the five venues - Forest Hills, Loretta Ingraham and Port Tampa - list a gymnasium among their features on the recreation-centres page, and Barksdale and Cordelia B Hunt do not; the City says nothing about the floor at any of them. Port Tampa's court was, on the City's page, "closed for repairs through June 1", the only statement about a court's condition anywhere in the city.

The Tampa pickleball scene

Fifty-two verified courts across sixteen venues, forty-five of them outdoors and seven inside community-centre buildings, every one counted on a single City page dated 28 October 2025. Macfarlane Park's eight is the largest published set; Cuscaden Park and Julian B Lane Riverfront Park hold six each; five venues hold four; the rest hold one or two. Tampa is the second Florida city on this site after Cape Coral and the first in Hillsborough County, and it is one of the largest cities on this site where the operator states indoor or outdoor for every published venue. It also has more published indoor venues than any city here, though Bellevue has more indoor courts, which is why Tampa has an indoor page, one of only a few on the site. What the City does not state is as much a part of the record as what it does: no lighting for any venue, no price for any court, no surface anywhere, and hours for only two parks and the pavilion. The "free beginner lessons" on Monday afternoons at Barksdale price a lesson, not a court, so no Tampa venue carries a fee answer. Three venues the City lists are not published, and they are named here rather than dropped. Rowlett Park has "8 courts", tied for the largest count in Tampa, at "2401 E Yukon St." - an address neither the Census address file nor OpenStreetMap can find. Foster Park has two courts at "4700 S Clark Ave., 33611", which the Census geocoder answers as North Clark Avenue in 33614 and OpenStreetMap cannot find as written. The Dr Martin Luther King Jr. Complex has two indoor courts with a schedule the City marks as not yet set and two City house numbers - 220 N Oregon Ave on the pickleball page, 2200 on the recreation-centres page - and the count-bearing one resolves to 33606 while the City prints 33607 on both pages.
