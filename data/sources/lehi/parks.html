Address: 6363 N. Fox Canyon Road, Lehi UT 84043

Joseph D. Adams Memorial Park

Address: 1830 N. Trinnaman Ln, Lehi UT 84043

Joseph D. Adams Memorial Park is a beautiful, neighborhood park located at the northern gateway of Lehi. Formerly known as North Entrance Park, the space was dedicated and renamed in August 2011 to honor the memory and legacy of Lehi Police Sergeant Joseph D. Adams, who gave his life in the line of duty on August 3, 2001. Today, the park serves as both a peaceful place of reflection for the community and an inviting green space for local families.
