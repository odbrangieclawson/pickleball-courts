Pickleball Complexes

The City of Albuquerque has two dedicated pickleball complexes: Manzano Mesa Pickleball Complex, and Villella Pickleball Courts. And two shared complexes at Sierra Vista Courts with 2 permanent pickleball courts, and 4 lined pickleball courts, and Pat Hurley Park with 4 dedicated and 4 blended lines.  

Manzano Mesa, Villella, and Pat Hurley are open from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.
Courts at Sierra Vista can be reserved in advanced by calling 505-767-5445. 

Availability & Reservations:

Courts are typically available on a first come, first serve basis and play is limited to 1.5 hours when others are waiting.

All reservations of pickleball courts and complexes for special events & tournaments are conducted through the City of Albuquerque's Parks & Recreation Sports Office.

Groups hosting non-City sponsored tournaments will need to have a Facility Use Agreement on file yearly with the City Sports Office. Liability Insurance also is required.

More information about reservations:

2026 Pickleball Lessons

Lesson registration can be made online at play.cabq.gov! Classes will be limited to six participants. Staff at Jerry Cline Tennis Center are also happy to help people who cannot register online.

Sign Up for Pickleball Lessons

For more information about pickleball lessons, call:

  • Jerry Cline Tennis Facility
    505-224-6682

Pickleball Class Descriptions

  • Junior Basics: Juniors will be learning basic movement, knowledge and have an understanding of the pickleball court and play.
  • Adult Basics: Adults will be learning basic movement, knowledge and have an understanding of the pickleball court and play.
  • Intermediate Play: Learning about more advanced skills and strategies, and have a better knowledge of movement around the pickleball court.

Pickleball lessons for juniors and adults will take place at Manzano Mesa Pickleball Complex.
