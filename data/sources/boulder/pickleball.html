Pickleball Classes and Leagues

Pickleball Instruction

Are you new to pickleball? Maybe you are an experienced player wanting to improve your skills? No matter your level of experience join us for pickleball classes!

  • Beginner classes: (Ages 16+) If you've never played pickleball before, this is designed for you! Taught by experienced instructors, you will learn the basics of pickleball rules, terminology, primary skills, coordination, beginner court time, and more!
  • Intermediate classes: (Age 16+) Continue to develop your game and learn some skills and drills from experienced instructors. This class is focused on improving your techniques and learning about new shots. You’ll be surprised how quickly you improve and how much fun you’ll have while doing it!

Pickleball Leagues

General league information

  • 2 play at once, any gender combination. 3 person roster max
  • Teams will play 1 match per night, for up to 1 hour.
  • Matches played at the SBRC or NBRC.
  • Leagues run for 7 weeks, ending with a best-match tournament.

Indoor Pickleball

Drop-in pickleball is offered inside our recreation center basketball gymnasiums. Indoor play is only available during the designated drop-in pickleball times, listed below, and pickleball nets are not available outside listed drop-in hours.

East Boulder Recreation Center

  • Monday: 1 to 3 pm and 6 to 8 pm on 3 indoor courts
  • Sunday: 2 to 4 pm on 3 indoor courts

South Boulder Recreation Center

  • Sunday: 9 to 11 am on 3 indoor courts*
  • Tuesday, Thursday: 1:30 to 3:30 pm on 3 indoor courts*

*There is no indoor Pickleball at South Boulder Recreation Center from June through October

North Boulder Recreation Center

  • Monday, Wednesday, Friday: 7:30 to 10:30 am on 3 indoor courts

Indoor drop-in pickleball is included with your daily entry fee or pass. Entry fees and pass options

Outdoor Pickleball Drop-in Schedule
