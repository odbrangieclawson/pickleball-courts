Pickleball

Pickleball is a sport that combines elements of tennis, badminton, and ping pong, making it accessible and enjoyable for people of all ages and skill levels.

The Town offers open-play pickleball courts at many of our parks. Due to the popularity of these amenities, courts cannot be reserved by players or groups other than by Apex Parks, Recreation and Cultural Resources Department for their sponsored programs. Organized league play and private lessons outside of any Town programming are not currently allowed per Town Ordinance.

Apex Parks, Recreation and Cultural Resources does offer pickleball programming. Check out the Program Guides for more information. Our Senior 55+ Indoor Pickleball Calendar is available here.
