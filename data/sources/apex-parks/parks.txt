Apex Community Park

This 160 acre park offers athletic fields and courts, picnic areas, playgrounds, fitness and nature trails, and a 50+ acre lake.

We're renovating part of this park - scroll down for project updates!

Park Details
Park Entrance: 2200 Laura Duncan Road
Lake Trail Entrance: 1808 Lake Pine Drive
Phone: (919) 249-3402
Hours of Operation: 6:30 am - 10:00 pm (year round)

Park Features
2 Street Hockey Rinks
3 sand volleyball courts
6 tennis courts
3 lighted pickleball courts
Elevate Fitness Course
2 basketball courts
3 ball fields and 2 batting cages (reserved for league play)
Playground and swings (ages 2-12)
Restroom facilities
3+ miles of developed nature and fitness trails (2 miles of paved greenway, 1 mile natural walking trail)
Large and Small Shelters
Picnic Areas (with grills)
Fishing dock (fishing permit required) - Apex Community Park is part of the NC Wildlife Community Fishing Program and is stocked with channel catfish. This pier is wheelchair accessible.
Vessel launch (vessel permit required) - Canoes, kayaks, paddleboards, sailboards, john boats and rowing skulls are allowed on the lake and must be launched from the designated launch site only. Vessels may not exceed 14ft in length.

Apex Community Park Playground Renovation Project
This project provides design and engineering services to enhance ADA accessibility at the playground adjacent to the baseball fields. The improvements will replace existing play equipment with new, inclusive features and incorporate ADA-accessible ramps and walkways connecting the playground to adjacent parking lots.
Status (8/20/2026) Current Status: Design phase. Project Lead: Town of Apex, Angela Reincke. Timeline: Anticipated completion TBD. Funding Source: General Fund.

John M. Brown Community Center

The John M. Brown Community Center (formerly known as Apex Community Center) is located on the Town Hall Campus at 53 Hunter Street. The center has several rooms available for rent. The three largest rooms are separated by partition walls that can open to accommodate the need for more than one room's capacity.

Hours for rentals must coincide with the building's operating hours:
Monday through Friday: 8am - 9pm
Saturday: 9am - 6pm
Sunday: 1pm - 6pm (Closed Sundays June through Labor Day)

Reservations can be processed in person at the John M. Brown Community Center between the hours of 8am-5pm, Monday through Friday. For information about availability, call (919) 249-3402.

SECTION 15-7 Code of Ordinances Town of Apex, North Carolina
(X) No person or group of persons shall use a Town of Apex public park or greenway for any activity of an organized athletic league or for an organized fundraising or commercial activity, without first obtaining a permit from the Town Parks, Recreation, and Cultural Resources Department. Notwithstanding the previous sentence, a person or group of persons is not required to obtain a permit from the Town Parks, Recreation, and Cultural Resources Department to engage in activities which are sponsored or co-sponsored by the Town of Apex.

Apex Community Park - Apex Community Park is a 160 acre park with multiple fields and courts as well as miles of developed nature and fitness trails. The park also has several playgrounds and an agility course.
Apex Jaycee Park - You'll find a youth-sized cricket pitch and ball games here, along with a batting cage and playground.
Apex Nature Park & Seymour Athletic Fields - One side of this park features a playground, athletic fields and courts, while the other side includes a dog park, disc golf course and outdoor amphitheater.
Clairmont Park - This 1.5 acre park offers a basketball court, picnic area, and playground.
Hunter Street Park - Home to a dog park, and Trackside Skate Plaza, this park also features soccer, softball, and a rentable shelter.
Kelly Glen Park - This 2 acre park offers a picnic area, and playground.
Kelly Road Park - This park features the popular KidsTowne playground, a softball field, tennis courts and trailhead to Beaver Creek Greenway.
Pleasant Park - Apex's newest park is a 92-acre facility with multi-purpose fields, several types of courts, a dreamy 1.5 acre Enchanted Forest playground with a splashpad, and so much more.
Salem Pond Park - Adjacent to Salem Middle and Elementary Schools, this park features two ponds, a lighted multi-purpose field and a walking trail.
Seagroves Farm Park - This 11+ acre park offers a walking trail around a pond, basketball court, picnic area, and playground.
Sue Helton Park - This .25 acre park offers a gazebo, playground, picnic table and grill.
West Street Park - This 1.4 acre park offers a basketball court, picnic area, and playground.
