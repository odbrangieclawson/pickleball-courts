Settlers Park

3245 N. Meridian Road, Meridian

Amenities & Features

Settlers Park is a 58-acre regional park built in phases between 2003 and 2014. It is located at the intersection of Meridian and Ustick Roads in Meridian.

  • Three Picnic Shelters
  • Bike Racks
  • Drinking Fountains
  • Mutt Mitt Stations
  • Off-Street Parking
  • Open Play Areas
  • Pathway
  • Stocked Fishing Pond
  • Universally Accessible Playground
  • Splash Pad
  • Climbing Rocks
  • Sound Garden
  • Tree of Hope
  • Shade Structures
  • Tables and Benches
  • Trash Receptacles
  • Softball Complex
  • Tennis and Pickleball Courts
  • Horseshoe Pits
  • Cornhole Courts
  • Concession Stand
  • Seasonal 9-Hole Disc Golf Course (Nov. - Feb.)
  • Bike Repair Station

Picnic Shelters Available to Rent:

Note: During times when there is not a paid reservation on a picnic shelter, it may be used for free on a first-come first-served basis.

Picnic shelters may not be reserved or occupied between 2:00 and 4:00 pm daily (with the exception of full-day reservations), as this is when crews come through to clean the shelters.

Shelter #1 

(Largest shelter located near multi-use athletic fields)

Maximum Capacity:
200 (generally 12 picnic tables)
Rental fee:
$98 (tax included) per 5-hour time block (9am-2pm or 4-9pm)

Rental includes access to power and use of free charcoal grill

Shelter #2

(Medium shelter located near splash pad & concession stand)

Maximum Capacity:
100 (generally 12 picnic tables)
Rental fee:
$78 (tax included) per 5-hour time block (9am-2pm or 4-9pm)

Rental includes access to power and use of free charcoal grill

Shelter #3

(Small shelter located near horseshoe pits/cornhole courts)

Maximum Capacity:
50 (Generally 4 picnic tables)
Rental fee:
$65 (tax included) per 5-hour time block (9am-2pm or 4-9pm)

Rental includes access to power and use of free charcoal grill

Settlers Park has:

  • Four reservable multi-use athletic fields
  • Seven reservable full-sized tennis courts
  • 16 non-reservable horseshoe courts
  • 5 reservable cornhole boards
  • 8 youth baseball/softball fields used by MYB
    • BASEBALL/SOFTBALL FACILITY SCHEDULE

Tennis & Pickleball Courts

Settlers Park has 10 Total Courts: 

  • Courts 1-7 are lined for tennis.
  • Courts 1-6 and 8-10 are lined for pickleball.
  • Courts 3-6 are lined for use with your own pickleball net.
  • Courts 5,6,8,9 and 10 are non-reservable.
  • Courts 1-4 & 7 are reservable at $8 per hour.

Reservation season is March 1 - October 31 annually. Courts are always open first-come first-served when there is not a paid reservation in place. Public Use with Rotation. Players need to share the court when there is a paddle line of players waiting.

Multi-Use Athletic Fields

Settlers Parks multi-use athletic fields can be reserved at a rate of $15 hour.
