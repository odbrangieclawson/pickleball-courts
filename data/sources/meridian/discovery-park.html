Discovery Park

2121 E. Lake Hazel Road, Meridian

Amenities & Features

 

Discovery Park, a 64-acre regional park established in 2019, is located in south Meridian along E. Lake Hazel Road, between Eagle and Locust Grove Roads. At approximately 64 acres in size, this park boasts several of Meridian's most unique and exciting amenities.

  • Playground
  • Splashpad
  • Sand and Water Play Area
  • Dual Zip Lines
  • Roller Slides
  • Climbing Wall
  • Idaho-Themed Interpretive Panels
  • Selfie Station
  • Toddler Play Structure
  • Pickleball and Tennis Courts
  • Bocce Ball Courts
  • 40-Yard Dash Challenge
  • Basketball Courts
  • Skatepark
  • Sand Volleyball Court
  • Hammock Poles
  • Shelter with WiFi
  • Bike Park and Pump Track
  • Softball Fields
  • Public Art
  • Benches
  • Picnic Shelters
  • Restrooms
  • Seasonal Concession Stand
  • Pathways
  • Off-Leash Dog Park
  • Covered Performance Stage with Lawn Seating
  • Bike Repair Station

(Please note that Discovery Park’s bike park, pump track, and skatepark will be closed during wet or inclement weather that might impact safety or the integrity of these amenities.)

Picnic Shelters Available to Rent:

Note: During times when there is not a paid reservation on a picnic shelter, it may be used for free on a first-come first-served basis.

Picnic shelters may not be reserved or occupied between 2:00 and 4:00 pm daily (with the exception of full-day reservations), as this is when crews come through to clean the shelters.

Tennis & Pickleball Courts

Discovery Park has 2 Tennis Courts which are dual striped for pickleball.
Court 1 is non-reservable.
Court 2 is reservable at $8 per hour.

Discovery Park has 6 Pickleball Courts. 
Courts 1,2,3 are non-reservable.
Courts 4-6 are reservable at $5 per hour.
Reservation season is March 1 - October 31 annually.
Courts are always open first-come first-served when there is not a paid reservation in place. Public Use with Rotation. Players need to share the court when there is a paddle line of players waiting.

Softball Fields

Discovery Park has four illuminated adult slow-pitch softball fields. Fields #3 and #4 will be reservable beginning 6/1/2024 at a rate of $15 per hour. To have a field illuminated will result in an additional charge. 

Discovery Park also has one non-illuminated youth fast-pitch/senior softball field which will be reservable beginning 6/1/2024 at a rate of $15 per hour.
