Julius M. Kleiner Memorial Park

1900 N. Records Avenue, Meridian

Amenities & Features:

Julius M. Kleiner Memorial Park is a 58-acre regional park established in 2012. The park was a gift to the City of Meridian in honor of the man who once farmed the land on which the park is now located.

  • Bandshell/Amphitheatre
  • Half-Court Basketball
  • Bike Racks
  • Bocce Ball
  • Center at the Park (Meridian Senior Center)
  • Community Garden and Pollinator Garden
  • Concession Stand
  • Drinking Fountains
  • Interpretive Displays
  • Labyrinth
  • Obelisks
  • Public Art
  • Off-Street Parking
  • Open Play Areas
  • Pathway
  • Picnic Shelters (5)
  • Playground
  • Stocked Fishing Ponds
  • Accessible Fishing Dock
  • Restrooms
  • Rock of Honor Veterans Memorial
  • Rolling Hills
  • Sand Volleyball
  • Pickleball Courts
  • Splash Pad
  • Statue of the Late Julius M. Kleiner
  • Tot Lots
  • Tree Arboretum
  • Memorial Plaza with Engraved Pavers
  • Seasonal 9-Hole Disc Golf Course
  • Trash Receptacles
  • Bike Repair Station

Picnic Shelters Available to Rent:

Note: During times when there is not a paid reservation on a picnic shelter, it may be used for free on a first-come first-served basis.

Picnic shelters may not be reserved or occupied between 2:00 and 4:00 pm daily (with the exception of full-day reservations), as this is when crews come through to clean the shelters.

Shelter A-1

(just south of the recreation complex)

Shelter Capacity:
400 (generally 25 picnic tables)
Reservation Time Blocks:
9am - 2pm / 4pm - 9pm
Rental Fee:
$195 (tax included) per 5-hour time block

A propane, industrial-sized barbecue griddle is available to rent per event date ($100 plus tax) in Shelter A-1.
Rental includes access to power and use of free charcoal grill.

Shelter A-2

(just north of the recreation complex - playground & splash pad area)

Shelter Capacity: 
100 (generally 8 picnic tables)
Reservation Time Blocks:
9am - 2pm / 4pm - 9pm
Rental Fee:
$98 (tax included) per 5-hour time block

Rental includes access to power and use of free charcoal grill
