Reta Huskey Park

2887 Tubac Drive Meridian, ID 83646

Amenities & Features

Reta Huskey Park, a 9-acre neighborhood park established in 2017, is located off N. Ten Mile Road in Meridian’s Bellano Creek subdivision. The park is named after Reta Huskey, a woman who grew up on the property, along with her five siblings, when it was a 120-acre ranch. It includes several design elements that pay tribute to Reta Huskey’s devotion to her family and her love of flowers.

  • Pathway
  • Playground
  • Sport Court
  • Picnic Shelter/Restroom Combo
  • Climbing Items
  • Half-Court Basketball
  • Three Non-Reservable Pickleball Courts
  • Outdoor Gym
  • Bike Repair Station

Picnic Shelter Available to Rent:

Maximum capacity: 30 (generally 4 picnic tables)
Rental Fee: $52 (includes tax) per time 9-hour time block (Noon – 9:00 pm)
Rental includes access to power and use of free charcoal grill.

Important: Please note that due to temporary staffing limitations, this shelter is only reservable Monday through Thursday. It may be used on a first-come first-served basis on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays.

Note: During times when there is not a paid reservation on a picnic shelter, it may be used for free on a first-come first-served basis.

Reta Huskey Park has one reservable multi-use athletic field.

Pickleball Courts

Reta Huskey Park has 3 pickleball courts that are non-reservable.

Courts are always open first-come first-served. Public use with rotation. Players need to share the courts when there is a paddle line of people waiting.
