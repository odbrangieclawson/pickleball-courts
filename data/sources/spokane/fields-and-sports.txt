Fields and Sports Facilities

The City of Spokane Parks & Recreation Department manages the athletic field scheduling for the City of Spokane to assure quality maintenance, equitable distribution and maximum use by the public, and when necessary, protection of fields from overuse.

Individuals or groups requesting rental of athletic fields for games or practices must complete the Field Allocation Process, as detailed below.

=== SECTION: Tennis and Pickleball Courts ===

Throughout the city parks system are many tennis courts that host neighborhood pick-up games, parks and recreation classes and many tennis events.
Tennis & Pickleball courts are first-come-first-served, however if you would like to officially reserve a court and receive a reservation permit please fill out the online form.

Field Reservations

Athletic fields at Sport Complexes (Dwight Merkel, Franklin, Southeast), Outlying Parks, and Spokane Public Elementary schools are available to researve. If a field is not researved, field usage becomes first-come first-served.

To request a reservation of an athletic field, please fill out this form.

*this form must be submitted a minimum of seven (7) full business days prior to your request date.

For questions, please contact: athleticfields@spokanecity.org or 509.625.6208.
