Oak Haven Park

NOTE: Alcohol is prohibited. For more information, view park safety and rules.

Featured Amenities

All amenities are first come, first served with the exception of the ones available for rental.

Little Free Library

Take a book or leave a book. This is a free community resource: donations and volunteers are welcome.

Pickleball

There is one outdoor pickleball court.

Address
16400 Parkstone
San Antonio, TX 78232
Directions

Tennis Court

The tennis court is lighted until 9 p.m.

Trails

Type: Asphalt / Length (Miles): 0.47

The lights for basketball and tennis courts turn off at 9 p.m. as part of a pilot program that was initiated with Council District 9, Oak Haven Neighborhood Association and Parks and Recreation.
