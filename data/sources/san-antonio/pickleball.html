Pickleball

There are several indoor and outdoor pickleball courts at our parks across the city. These are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Some courts are not lighted.

  • Courts at Hamilton Community Center are available Saturday, 10 a.m. - 2 p.m.
  • All other courts are available during park hours.

Want to learn how to play? Take a Pickleball 101 class through the Fitness in the Park program, equipment provided.
