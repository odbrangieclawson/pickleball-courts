Fairchild Park

NOTE: Alcohol is prohibited. For more information, view park safety and rules.

Featured Amenities

All amenities are first come, first served.

Pickleball Courts

There are 18 lighted, outdoor pickleball courts.

Swimming Pool

Closed until the 2027 pool season.

Phone: 210-207-5357

Tennis Courts

There are 10 lighted, outdoor tennis courts.

Tennis & Pickleball Court Hours

All tennis courts and pickleball courts are open during park hours.

Office Hours: Monday - Thursday: 4 - 8 p.m.

NOTE: Special events and scheduled activities may affect court usage and open hours. Please call 210-226-6912 for more information.
