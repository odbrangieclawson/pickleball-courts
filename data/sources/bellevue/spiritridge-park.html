Fall Into Fun

Fall Into Fun is a month-long celebration of connection, belonging and community. Free and low-cost activities activities for all ages and abilities include dance, swimming, karaoke, cultural experiences and more! Browse the Fall Into Fun webpage or event calendar to plan your activities throughout September.

From I-90, take 156th Avenue SE exit. Turn right on Eastgate Way. Turn left on 161st Avenue SE. Turn right on SE 33rd Place.
