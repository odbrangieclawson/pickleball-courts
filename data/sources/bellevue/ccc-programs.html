  • Parkside Pre-K

Header Image

Crossroads Community Center offers many programs and partners with community providers to serve anyone interested in recreational activities. We offer a range of afterschool programming, pre-kindergarten, cultural growth, and health & wellness programs. Crossroads Community Center is a welcoming environment for life-long learning for all. 

CCC Programs, Activities, Services & More

View our full catalogue of classes, programs, activities, services, and events with our online registration system. From fitness classes to art workshops and special events, there's something for everyone. Explore and sign up today!

Financial Assistance

The City of Bellevue offers financial assistance for Bellevue residents for most Parks & Community Services programs.  Visit the Financial Assistance webpage or call 425-452-6885 for more information. 

Games Room

Join the fun at the Crossroads Community Center Games Room! The room includes two Table Tennis tables, four Pool Tables, Air Hockey, Pop-a-Shot, Giant Connect Four, Table Top XL Jenga and 16 fun table games to choose from including Mah Jong, chess, Sling Hockey, Shut the Box, decks of cards and more.  Participants are welcome to use the games room during business hours. 

Bring your friends and family, make new friends, socialize, and sharpen your mind!

Drop-In Activities

We offer a variety of drop-in activities for all ages and abilities. Join us for table tennis, pickleball, basketball, volleyball, or indoor kite flying! View the schedule for a full list of activities. 

No registration required: first-come, first-served. Space is limited.

Gamerspace

  • Thursdays, Sept. 3-Dec. 17
  • 4-6 p.m.
  • Ages: 13-18
  • FREE

Participants are welcome to come at any point between the start and end times and stay for as long as they like.

The CCC Gamerspace includes party games for the Nintendo Switch 2 (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Mario Kart World, Minecraft, etc.), board games (Chess, Settlers of Catan, etc.), tabletop games (Dungeons and Dragons), and trading card games (Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon).
