Black Mountain Recreation Center/Aquatic Complex is a 41,000 square foot facility serving the community by offering a diverse array of programming and special events designed for people of all ages.

Black Mountain's warm, welcoming atmosphere provides a sense of belonging and familiarity. Staff members greet patrons by name and enjoy working closely with them to find just the right program or activity to suit their needs.

During the summer months, Black Mountain Aquatic Complex is a favorite gathering place for neighborhood families to enjoy the loop slide, waterfall and sunshine.

Catering to families, Silver Springs offers a wide array of center-based events and activities as well as high quality early childhood programs. During the summer, families can also enjoy Silver Springs Outdoor Swimming Pool.
