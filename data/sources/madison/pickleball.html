Pickleball

Among the nearly 100 tennis courts in Madison, many are dual-striped for pickleball. Dual-striped courts use a tennis net which is about 2" taller than a standard pickleball net. Garner Park offers Madison's only dedicated outdoor pickleball courts.

While the courts are available for individuals on a first-come, first-served basis, all leagues and lessons must reserve in advance.

Reserve Outdoor Pickleball Court

Indoor Pickleball Courts

With the expansion of Warner Park Community Recreation Center, we now offer indoor pickleball courts. See WPCRC to learn more and reserve.
