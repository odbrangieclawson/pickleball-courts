Fitness Center & Pickleball Membership

The WPCRC's all-new fitness and pickleball court memberships and passes are designed to offer you the best affordable access! The new fitness center space overlooks Warner Park and features all-new flooring, new equipment, and classic favorites. New indoor pickleball courts are open and ready to be reserved. 

Memberships and Passes

Annual and 6-month membership and 20-visit passes are available and include use of the fitness center and pickleball courts. 

Use Guidelines

  • A WPCRC ID card is required.
  • Wear non-marking rubber-soled shoes with soft, flat soles that won't scuff or damage indoor gym floors.
  • No food or beverages allowed. Water bottles are acceptable. 

Youth

Youth, ages 12-17, must be accompanied by an adult.  Youth under 12 are not allowed at any time in the fitness center. 

 

All-New Fitness Center

Featuring treadmills, ellipticals, NuStep, recumbent and upright bikes, and rowers, plus TRX suspension trainer, multi-strength trainer, dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls and mats.

Indoor Pickleball Courts

To play pickleball, an active WPCRC ID and Fitness Center/Pickleball Membership is required. If you don't have a Fitness Center Membership, you're welcome to play by paying a Daily Admission and an active WPCRC ID is still required.

Blue Gym: 2 courts - 20ft x 44ft
Green Gym: 3 courts - 20ft x 44ft

Reserve Indoor Pickleball Court

Pickleball Court Reservation Checklist
  1. Log into your WebTrac Account: 
    • If you already have an account in WebTrac, please log in. 
    • If you do not have an account, you will need to create one. 
    • While creating an account, the system checks for duplicate accounts. If you receive a notice that your information is duplicated, click the Reactivate WebTrac Account button for assistance.
    • Once you're logged into your account, confirm your household members and add new family members, as needed.
  2. To play pickleball, an active WPCRC ID and Fitness Center/Pickleball Membership is required. If you don't have a Fitness Center Membership, you're welcome to play by paying a Daily Admission; an active WPCRC ID is still required.
  3. Once you have your WPCRC ID and Membership, you're ready to reserve a court -- All players are required to have a WPCRC ID and Membership, one participant reserves the court.
  4. Go to cart and purchase.
  5. Check in at the front desk and pick up your ID card (if needed).

If you have any questions, please contact WPCRC at 608-245-3670.
