Giuffrida Park

This 3.7-acre park was the first neighborhood park in Cape Coral and offers a nice setting for small gatherings, playing basketball, and letting kids enjoy the playground. 

Note: Giuffrida Park underwent several park improvements in 2021-2022 covered by the Parks GO Bond. Click here to learn about GO Bond improvement projects to existing parks.

PARK AMENITIES:

  • Basketball Courts
  • Picnic Pavilions with BBQ Grills*
  • Two Pickleball Courts
  • Playground Equipment (for ages 2-12)
  • Multi-Use Path
  • Restrooms
