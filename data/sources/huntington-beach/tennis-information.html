OC Racquet Sports
(760) 902-0686 - (also the rain hotline)
www.ocracquetsports.com

Serve up your skills with OC Racquet Sports. Our clinics are designed for players of all ages and skill levels, from beginners to advanced competitors. Led by our experienced and passionate coaches, our clinics offer a unique opportunity to improve your technique, strategy, and overall performance on the court. With a focus on small group instruction, you'll receive personalized attention and feedback to help you reach your tennis or pickleball goals. Join our vibrant community of racquet enthusiasts and take advantage of Huntington Beach’s premier instructors. Whether you're aiming to compete at a high level or simply enjoy the sport, our clinics and lessons will help unlock your full potential on the court. Enroll today and let us help you smash your way to success!

CLINICS -  Classes are a great way to take your game to the next level. OC Racquet Sports has over 50 classes offered each week. We have a class for every player and level. We believe that learning in a fun-filled environment is the best way to maximize your abilities with the guidance of top-level instructors.

CAMPS - Camps are a great way to make a huge leap in your game with 4.5 hours of daily instruction. Camps are available during the holidays and summer season.

PRIVATE LESSONS - OC Racquet Sports also instructs private and semi-private lessons for all players.

Why Play Tennis?

Tennis is known as a "sport for a lifetime". Not only is tennis a great workout, you can gain speed, eye-hand coordination, flexibility, agility, balance, and much more. Tennis is a great sport to start early in life but can just as easily be an excellent sport to pick up later in life as well, but most importantly Tennis is FUN! Develop friendships, get outdoors, and have fun while experiencing the competitiveness and physical challenges that truly make tennis a well rounded sport.
