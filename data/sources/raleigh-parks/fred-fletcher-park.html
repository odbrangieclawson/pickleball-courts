Fred Fletcher Park

Updated: Jul 14, 2026

Jump To:

 
Water Garden and Wetland Garris Building Borden Building and Amphitheater Shelters Courts Fields Playground

Year 1981 | Acres 21.4

The park is the former campus of the Methodist Home for Children and was purchased by the City in 1982. There are entrances to the park off of St. Mary's Street and Glenwood Avenue. Enjoy a stroll around the walking paths throughout the park. Parking is available at 820 Clay Street and 805 Washington Street.

Water Garden and Wetland

  • An aesthetically pleasing garden with a variety of evergreen and perennial plant species
  • Four deep pools capture sediment, nutrients, and other pollutants as they drift to the bottom of the pools when stormwater flows
  • Ecosystem for this area of the park for butterflies, dragonflies, frogs, ducks, and other animal species
  • Naturally filtering stormwater that flows into the water garden to clean the runoff before flowing downstream into Pigeon House Branch
  • Learn about the Fred Fletcher Park Wetland Repairs Project

Garris Building

  • Serves as the business office for School Programs, Nature Programs, and Teen Programs.
