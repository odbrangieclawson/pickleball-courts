Courts

  • Walk-up play is first-come, first-served.
  • Walkup use is not allowed on courts scheduled for a Raleigh Parks league or programmed activities.
  • The scheduled activity has priority court usage over any alternative walk-up use.

Playgrounds

The playgrounds at Baileywick have a wood chip surface and are designed for children ages 2-5 and 5-12.

Dog Park

Welcome to the Baileywick dog park! It's where tails wag and friendships bloom. We have small and big dog areas! Come join the fun!

Learn More about our Dog Park Rules

Greenway

The .6-mile Baileywick Greenway Trail connects from Strickland Rd to Baileywick Rd by passing through Baileywick Park. It connects to Baileywick Park and Baileywick Elementary School. 

Parking is at 9501 Baileywick Rd, Raleigh, NC 27615 Lot
