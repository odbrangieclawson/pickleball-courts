Neighborhood Center

Powell Drive Neighborhood Center is an unstaffed facility open only during rentals and/or events and classes.

  • Take a virtual tour of the neighborhood center.
  • Request your rental online using RecLink or by reaching out to the facility.
  • Non-Resident Rental Fee: A 25% fee applies to the hourly rental rate for non-residents. This fee does not apply to security deposits, staffing fees, or other additional charges.
  • Take note: Requests will be subject to staff approval. Upon approval, payment will be required to confirm the reservation. Your reservation is not finalized until you receive confirmation of your payment.

Meeting Room

  • Overlooking the back patio and playground
  • Capacity: 75
  • Includes use of full-size kitchen
  • $65/hr. (2-hour minimum)

Warming Kitchen

Food may only be warmed and not prepared from scratch.

Courts

Basketball Court

Outdoor court with a concrete surface

Tennis Courts

  • 2 courts
  • Lighting is on a timer from dusk to 10 p.m. (March to November)
  • No backboard

Courts are typically available on a first-come, first-served basis.

View other court locations.

Shelters

Request your rental online using RecLink or by reaching out to the facility.

Take note: Requests will be subject to staff approval. Upon approval, payment is required to confirm the reservation. Your reservation is not finalized until you receive confirmation of your payment.

Shelters # 1-3

  • Capacity: 15–35
  • 1 table
  • No grill
  • $12/hr to rent
  • No electricity

View other shelter locations.

Playground

  • The playground for ages 2–12 years has a rubberized safety surface, climbing structures with slides, and swings (both regular and tot).
  • Bathrooms are available near the playground in the comfort station.

View other playground locations.

Field

The multipurpose field is 225' × 150'.

Request your rental online using RecLink or contact Athletics at 919-996-6836.
