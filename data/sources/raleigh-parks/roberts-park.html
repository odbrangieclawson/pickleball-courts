Roberts Park

Updated: Jul 15, 2026

Jump To:

 
Community Center Rentals Shelters Courts Dog Run Field Playgrounds
  • Year 1956 | Acres 7.2
  • Roberts Park is located in the Battery Heights Historic District, which is known for its earthen batteries made during the Civil War.
  • Roberts Park resides on the former property of Bartholomew Gatling, county attorney and postal carrier from 1915 to 1922.

Fitness Room

  • Mat flooring
  • Up to 50 lbs. in dumbbells
  • $2 day pass or $15 monthly pass

Rentals

Request your rental online using RecLink or by reaching out to the facility. 

  • All rooms/spaces must be rented for a minimum of two hours.
  • There is a $15 application fee.
  • There is a $100 damage deposit required for all rentals. It may be refunded if the renter adheres to the contract and stays within the rented time.
  • There is an additional fee of $30 per hour for any indoor rentals that occur outside of operating hours.
  • Non-Resident Rental Fee: A 25% fee applies to the hourly rental rate for non-residents. This fee does not apply to security deposits, staffing fees, or other additional charges.

Take note: Requests will be subject to staff approval. Upon approval, payment will be required to confirm the reservation. Your reservation is not finalized until you receive confirmation of your payment.
