Available for Reservations:

  • Patio

Park Amenities

  • Los Olivos Community Center
  • 2 Restrooms
  • 4 Drinking Fountains
  • 1 Children's Play Area (Musical-Themed Playground)
  • 1 Open Play Area
  • 1 Lighted Baseball Field
  • 1 Lighted Soccer Field
  • 1 Lighted Basketball Court
  • 3 Lighted Tennis Courts
  • 3 Lighted Pickleball Courts
  • 2 Lighted Badminton Courts

  • 10 Barbecues
  • 2 Picnic Shelters
  • 8 Picnic Tables
