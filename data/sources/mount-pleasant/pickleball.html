Pickleball

Pickleball Instructional Clinics & Private Lessons

Instructional PB Clinics - The Deb Gym at Town Hall

Contact Kekoa Lee, 843-568-5703, for more information and to reserve your spot!

Space is limited to 8 people per clinic and is available on a first come, first served basis for sign ups.
Clinics are offered for ages 18+ 
Clinics are offered for anyone from beginners to advanced players looking to further develop their game.

CANCELLATIONS MUST BE MADE AT LEAST 24 HOURS PRIOR TO CLINIC!

Beginner Clinic - Have fun while learning the basics of Pickleball: Basic rules, etiquette, scoring, technique and strategy/concept for beginners.   

Intermediate Clinic (3.0-4.0) - You have been playing for awhile and you would like to get better. To be more competitive you will learn higher level techniques like the 3rd shot drop and blocking drives. Higher level strategy/concepts are taught here with an emphasis on shot selection. Let's also take your serve to the next level!

Advanced Clinic (4.0+) - For highly competitive players! Professional Coaching based around game nights: mini tournaments, round robins, king/queen of the court, etc... Each player will compete in the sessions set format for that night and receive coaching. Emphasis is on improving weaknesses and developing new weapons!

All payments must be made through MPRD.

Instructional Clinics at Miriam Brown

Meet Coach Don Fickling, PCI Certified Pickleball Coach:
Coach Don Fickling is a PCI Certified Pickleball Coach known for his extensive playing experience, creative instruction, and ability to help players of all skill levels to improve their game in a fun and supportive environment.

Don is assisted by Jackie Kelly, USA Pickleball Ambassador.  Together, they lead Old Blue Truck Pickleball, a community-focused initiative dedicated to growing the game and fostering a welcoming pickleball environment throughout the Town of Mount Pleasant.
