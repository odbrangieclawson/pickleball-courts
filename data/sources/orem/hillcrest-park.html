Hillcrest Park

Location: 650 E 1400 S

Hillcrest Park is a recreational area nestled in a picturesque location, boasting an array of amenities to cater to visitors of all ages. This park offers a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

One of the park’s highlights is the presence of three well-equipped playgrounds, ensuring that children have ample space to play and explore.

Park Amenities

Playgrounds: Nature Playground, Three Tier Playground, Toddler Playground.

12 Pickleball Courts (6 Courts reservable, 6 Courts open for public use)

Small Pavilions for temporary seating

Pavilion-Rentable Big Pavilion for Gatherings (Capacity: 75) 10 tables Reserve the Hillcrest Pavilion

The large pavilion at Hillcrest is reservable (Please visit our “Reserve a Pavilion” button on the main page of the parks website.

The Splash Pad is open seasonally from Memorial Day at 10:00am to Labor Day at 8pm. After Labor Day, it is closed until the following Memorial Day.

6 Pickleball courts can be reserved for private use

6 of the other courts are for public use and will be held to the standard of using the “Paddle Rotation System.” This means that waiting player place a paddle for each player on the paddle racks on the next open rack. When a game is finished players announce “Open Court” and the court vacated for the players with paddles on the rack.
