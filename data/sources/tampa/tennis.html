Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex

This tennis facility is home to 8 clay courts and a practice wall. This staffed facility is open Monday to Friday from 8 am to 9 pm and Saturday and Sunday from 8 am to 6 pm.

Wheelchair Tennis Clinic ¶

The Parks and Recreation Department offers a wheelchair tennis clinic/practice, every Monday from 7-9 pm. All equipment, including sports chairs, is available for use. Rec Card required. Please send e-mail to Andrew.Sheets@Tampa.gov for more information.
