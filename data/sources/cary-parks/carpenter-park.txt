CARPENTER PARK

4420 Louis Stephens Drive

This 16-acre neighborhood park features multiple pedestrian trails, a community garden, and is the memorial site for Flights AE3379 and AE3378.

Features
Central Pond with Earthen Dam
Restroom Structure
Children's Play Area
Play Lawn Area
Multi-Sport Courts: Basketball & 3 Pickleball Courts
Community Garden
Terraced Lawn Area
Preserved Forest
Pedestrian Trail
Parking for 30 Cars
Flight 3379 & 3378 Memorial
Picnic Shelter (first-come, first-served) at the playground

Park Hours
Sunrise to Sunset. In lighted areas where visitors are participating in an approved extended-use activity, hours are extended until 30 minutes after the activity ends or 11 p.m., whichever comes first.
