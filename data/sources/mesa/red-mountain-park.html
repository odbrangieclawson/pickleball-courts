Additional Features

  • 1,146.3 acres
  • Pickleball Court - Lines on Basketball court (requires personal net)
  • Shaded Playground
  • Pickleball Courts
    • Lines at basketball court requires personal net
    • Standalone courts have nets

The following areas at Red Mountain Park are available for reserving. You can find more information and reserve online by clicking Facility Reservations to the right.

Reservable Spaces

  • Ramadas
  • Multiuse Fields (by permit only)
    • Red Mountain Multiuse Fields North: 7765 E Brown Rd.
    • Red Mountain Multiuse Fields South: 905 N Sunvalley Rd.
  • Ballfields (by permit only)
    • Red Mountain Baseball Complex: 8008 E Brown Rd.
    • Red Mountain Softball East: 7808 E Brown Rd.
    • Red Mountain Softball West: 7624 E Brown Rd
