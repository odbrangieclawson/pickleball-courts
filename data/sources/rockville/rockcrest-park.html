Rockcrest Park

Features

Rockcrest Park is a 7.4-acre neighborhood park that connects various streets and areas in the Twinbrook Neighborhood. The park also contains the Rockcrest Ballet Center as well as a new for 2024 playground, basketball courts, pickleball courts (must bring own nets), and paved paths connecting Broadwood Avenue to Ardennes Avenue. The paved path system ultimately leads to the Twinbrook Community Center.
