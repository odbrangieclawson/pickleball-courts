=== SECTION: Court & Layout ===
Court & Layout A pickleball court measures 20 feet wide by 44 feet long for singles and doubles. The net is 36 inches high at the sidelines and 34 in the center. Each side has a 7-foot non-volley zone (“the Kitchen”), adding strategy and placement to play. Equipment
